# Product selection after lifting the universe restriction

Review dated **16 October 2026**. The user authorizes choosing products beyond the ETFs tested so far. Free data remains the first choice; daily or multi-day signals remain the working horizon. This review creates no provider account, subscription or brokerage connection, downloads no historical price series, and does not open the reserved **2023–2025 price paths**. It recommends the next research question on economic and implementation grounds, without screening candidate universes for historical returns.

The [first pilot](EDGE_PILOT.md) and [sector pilot](SECTOR_EDGE_PILOT.md) remain inconclusive. A larger universe does not strengthen those results. Universe, signal and parameter choices are part of the cumulative search count; unsuccessful product choices must remain recorded.

The coordinating decision adopts the [global weekly reversal protocol](GLOBAL_REVERSAL_PROTOCOL.md), frozen before acquiring its new histories: **SPY, IWM, QQQ, EFA, EEM, IEF, TLT, GLD and DBC**. These US-listed USD products cover several equity regions/styles, two Treasury durations, gold and commodity-futures exposure. The choice expands economic coverage while retaining a common session/calendar and explicit research accounting convention; it was not selected by comparing backtest returns. The requested window begins in 2007, initial development begins in 2008, and validation remains 2013–2022.

The adopted mechanism is weekly **relative reversal**: rank standardized recent declines and allocate equally to the lowest K scores. The four configurations are L in {1,5} and K in {1,3}, with **L5/K3** primary. Its 63-return volatility estimate ends before the L-session signal return, and targets execute at the following official session open. Fully invested ranking, lagged volatility, explicit holiday-week scheduling and prior-development allocation references differ materially from the threshold-triggered four-fund sleeve proposal below. Those choices are recorded in the separate declaration; the two designs must not be pooled or presented as one preregistered rule.

The four-fund proposal below was **considered but not adopted or run**. Retaining it records the research choice without adding its four unexecuted configurations to the count of measured backtests. The chosen weekly study adds four measured configurations if executed, bringing the known family to at least fourteen. Expanding the products does not remove the adjusted-index, retrospective-universe, cost or contamination limitations, and it does not justify opening the reserved prices.

All nine selected products have issuer-reported inception before the requested **3 January 2007** start. This verifies fund existence, not first observed trading date, uninterrupted vendor history, historical liquidity or an unchanged mandate. Product pages also display current statistics and summary returns; those are not selection inputs.

## Where a defensible experiment is practical

| Product family | Useful question | Observations and accounting required | Decision now |
|---|---|---|---|
| US-listed broad global equity ETFs | Does a multi-day decline predict a subsequent rebound after the next eligible entry and trading costs? | Completed US-session ETF bars/actions; inception/coverage checks; USD trading prices; next-open timing; overnight positions. The existing adjusted-index ledger can screen this, with its existing research-only limitation. | **Selected research mechanism**, broadened to the nine-fund protocol above. |
| US-listed Treasury, credit, real-estate, gold and commodity funds | Trend, duration/credit compensation, diversification or slow rebalancing | Identify each vehicle's structure and actual start. Bond/credit distributions, REIT events and commodity-fund rolls have distinct mechanisms. A futures-backed fund is not spot commodity exposure. | Treasury/gold/commodity exposures enter the frozen nine-fund question; credit and real estate are not added. Diversification alone is not predictive edge. |
| Individual stocks | Filing-linked underreaction, quality/value, short-term reversal | Historical eligibility and stable security identifiers; active and delisted prices; terminal proceeds; corporate actions; as-filed observations and availability times. A contemporary stock screener is insufficient. | Defer a broad historical cross-section until these data exist. A prospectively frozen stock cohort could support forward simulation, with limited generalizability. |
| Listed futures, including FX futures | Carry/term structure, diversified trend or roll-related effects | Contract-specific prices/settlements, multipliers, expiry/delivery rules, a predeclared roll, spreads, daily variation margin and collateral. Carry additionally needs the curve observable at each decision. | Defer implementation until a verified contract dataset and futures ledger exist. A continuous ticker cannot substitute for the traded contract ledger. |
| OTC spot FX | Currency trend or interest-rate carry | Venue-specific bid/ask observations, session boundaries, base/quote currencies, conversion marks and historical financing/rollover. Carry requires historical forward points or applicable funding rates. | Defer. Public central-bank reference rates support context and conversion checks, not executable FX fills or a funded carry backtest. |
| Crypto spot | Venue-specific multi-day persistence/reversal or market fragmentation | Explicit venue/pair, quote currency, 24-hour UTC boundaries, original trades or candles, venue outages, asset/pair inception and delisting, historical maker/taker fees. No stablecoin-to-USD equality assumption. | A credible later free-data branch, but needs a separate calendar and execution path. Do not silently route it through NYSE daily sessions. |
| Crypto perpetuals/options | Funding or volatility compensation | Funding and mark/index histories, changing contract specifications, liquidation/margin and expiry/exercise accounting, quotes and executable spreads. | Defer. Spot candles alone cannot test these mechanisms. |

CME explicitly distinguishes a continuous **settlement price series** from a rolling futures investment index. The continuous series maps active/front contracts; the rolling index specifies an investment roll. Neither lets us ignore traded contract changes or infer a free historical data entitlement. [CME continuous series](https://www.cmegroup.com/market-data/cme-group-continuous-price-series.html), [CME rolling-index explanation](https://www.cmegroup.com/articles/2025/a-guide-to-understanding-cme-group-rolling-futures-indices.html)

SEC's submissions/XBRL APIs need no key and provide useful original-filing references. They are not a complete stock-universe, delisting-price or point-in-time feature database. In particular, the frames endpoint chooses a last-filed fact for its requested calendar period; using that as an old decision's feature risks a later filing entering the past. Preserve accession-linked filings and filter by verified availability. [SEC API contracts](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces)

The ECB states that its reference rates are for information and discourages transaction use. Their observation/publication convention is different from a venue's executable bid/ask. [ECB framework](https://www.ecb.europa.eu/stats/pdf/exchange/Frameworkfortheeuroforeignexchangereferencerates.en.pdf)

## Considered alternative: four-fund threshold reversal

**Temporary price pressure may produce multi-day rebounds in broad equity ETFs.** The economic question differs from both previous momentum studies: does buying a sufficiently large recent decline earn a subsequent net return that survives a delayed entry, adverse costs and exposure attribution?

The prior literature connects short-term stock reversal with compensation for providing liquidity, with returns varying by market conditions. That evidence is neither proof that a long-only ETF implementation works nor permission to import a VIX filter after inspecting results. ETF arbitrage, rapid price discovery and the next-open delay may eliminate the effect. This is a falsifiable reason to test, with a plausible negative outcome. [Nagel, *Evaporating Liquidity*, original paper record](https://www.nber.org/papers/w17653)

The independent review initially proposed a fixed universe of **SPY, IWM, EFA and EEM**:

Inception is verified from issuer information; it does not certify the provider's first tradable session, complete history or liquidity throughout the requested period. That alternative proposed the same **2005–2022 development price boundary** as the first daily pilot, with 2005 for warm-up, 2006–2012 development and 2013–2022 validation. This cohort remains selected retrospectively. EFA/EEM trade in USD in US hours but embed foreign equity/FX exposures and different underlying-market closures. Signals and simulated fills must use the ETF's own US-session observations. An ETF price versus stale foreign NAV is not a measured arbitrage; USD listing does not remove currency risk.

The unadopted design used four independent, non-borrowing sleeves, one per fund:

- At each completed close, compute the trailing **L-session log return**, divided by `sqrt(L)` times the sample standard deviation of the last 20 completed daily log returns. L is 3 or 5. Require 21 closes, a finite positive denominator and a flat sleeve.
- If that standardized decline is at or below `−z`, propose investing the sleeve at the next session open. z is 1.0 or 1.5. Hold for five complete open-to-open session intervals, then exit at the pre-scheduled session open. Ignore new signals while the sleeve is occupied. Do not extend the holding period after seeing another decline.
- Primary candidate **L=5, z=1.0**, with the other three combinations retained as sensitivity. No trend, volatility-regime, VIX, price-recovery exit or stop rule is added after validation. Each sleeve receives USD25,000 of the shared USD100,000 initial capital and cannot borrow from another sleeve; total gross exposure remains at most one.
- Use identical **5/10/20 bps per-side cost scenarios**, completed observations and next-open conventions across strategies and passive references. Entry delayed by one additional session is a predeclared execution robustness view, not a new candidate to select when it looks better. This doubles the execution views, not the number of independent return paths.
- Compare against passive equal-capital exposure to the four funds, SPY and zero-yield cash. Report capital exposure, beta, conditional entry returns, turnover, costs and drawdown. Lower exposure can explain a lower drawdown; positive trading P&L alone is not excess-return evidence. An ex-post exposure-matched diagnostic must be labelled attribution, not an implementable benchmark known in advance.

This is a retained research alternative, **not the frozen study protocol**. No acquisition or experiment implements its sleeve rules. Do not implement it alongside the chosen weekly design and retain whichever is best. Product-level inspection, failed acquisition and cancellation remain visible in the adopted study.

## Free-source possibilities and boundaries

The installed yfinance path can acquire ETF daily bars/actions without adding accounts. It remains an unofficial research adapter with personal-use data restrictions, revised adjusted histories and no historical executable quote guarantee. Freeze and preserve settings, all returned columns, metadata, failed symbols and checksums. Do not repair missing observations or switch to a different fund/provider after seeing strategy returns. [Maintainer data-use statement](https://github.com/ranaroussi/yfinance)

Crypto is not ruled out by lack of free data. Coinbase's official candle contract advertises daily granularity, at most **300 candles** per request, potentially incomplete history and omitted no-tick buckets; it can also return buckets preceding `start`. Any future adapter must archive and classify those observations rather than fill gaps or assume complete pagination. Public candles are aggregated venue observations, not bid/ask or a market-wide consolidated tape. [Coinbase historical candles](https://docs.cdp.coinbase.com/api-reference/exchange-api/rest-api/products/get-product-candles)

Binance's official archive describes daily/monthly spot candle and trade files with SHA-256 sidecars and acknowledges later archive revisions. Its separate dataset terms, version 1.0 dated **26 August 2026**, specify **CC BY-NC-SA 4.0** and additional conditions, including non-commercial personal historical research and a separate written license for commercial use. The repository's software license is not a general market-data license. Document the exact terms/version before any acquisition or MCP distribution. [Archive specification](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data), [Dataset terms](https://github.com/binance/binance-public-data/blob/master/TERMS_AND_CONDITIONS.md)

## Evidence and operational acceptance

The recommendation can be screened now only through the explicitly labelled adjusted-index research proxy. Shorter holding periods make trading-cost and adjustment errors more material. Before any operational simulated strategy, independently check raw-share splits/distributions and payment cash, available sleeve cash, settlement assumptions, opening spreads/impact and the treatment of suspended/unavailable instruments. Every proposed position remains subject to deterministic host risk checks. A strategy cannot choose its own price, bypass missing data or spend proceeds before the declared cash convention allows it.

Acceptance for the next study is a fixed declaration before acquisition; complete immutable development observations or a failed-data report; known-answer entry/holding/cost fixtures; independent reconciliation; future-data perturbation and replay checks; recorded attempts and chronological validation; uncertainty and costs that can reject the hypothesis. A positive adjusted-index result remains inconclusive pending independent-source replication, the raw-share ledger, a locked final-test decision and genuinely forward simulated observations. MCP discovery/execution can expose research progress without labelling the strategy validated.

During this documentation review, official issuer pages/search snippets displayed current statistics and embedded historical summary-return tables, including EEM annual figures for the reserved years. Those figures were not used for universe or parameter selection, and no historical price path was acquired. This contextual exposure prevents a claim that the reserved years are globally unknown. The machine-enforced boundary must continue to state precisely that their **price series have not been requested or evaluated** under these studies.
