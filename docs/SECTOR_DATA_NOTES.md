# Sector ETF data and evidence review

Independent source and implementation review dated **16 October 2026**. This note supports the frozen [sector protocol](NEXT_EDGE_PROTOCOL.md); it does not change its four configurations, acquisition window or reserved period. It contains no sector-performance findings.

## Universe history verified from primary sources

State Street's December 2019 issuer publication, Figure 4, reports inception **16 December 1998** for each declared original fund: XLB, XLE, XLF, XLI, XLK, XLP, XLU, XLV and XLY. The same table reports XLRE inception **7 October 2015** and XLC inception **18 June 2018**. Fund inception does not certify a vendor's first daily trading observation or uninterrupted coverage. A 2000 start is after the original funds' inception, but exact requested sessions must still pass the dataset quality check. [Issuer historical table, page 3](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/pdfs/etf/us/target-a-lower-cost-of-ownership-with-sector-etfs.pdf)

The original nine are a fixed historical fund cohort, not an unchanging representation of all present-day sectors. S&P announced that equity real estate would leave Financials, with S&P index implementation after **16 September 2016**. Mortgage REITs remained in Financials. Later, the expanded Communication Services classification moved businesses previously classified in Technology and Consumer Discretionary. GICS Direct implementation was announced for after **28 September 2018**; individual index and fund implementation dates require their own records. These announcements justify explicit structural-change diagnostics, not retroactive replacement of ETF histories. [S&P 2016 announcement](https://press.spglobal.com/2016-03-08-S-P-Dow-Jones-Indices-And-MSCI-Revisions-To-The-Global-Industry-Classification-Standard-GICS-Structure), [S&P 2018 announcement](https://press.spglobal.com/2017-11-15-S-P-Dow-Jones-Indices-And-MSCI-Announce-Revisions-To-The-Global-Industry-Classification-Standard-GICS-R-Structure-In-2018)

**XLF's 2016 event needs a separate accounting check.** This was an in-kind distribution of XLRE shares to XLF holders, not just a new sector name. The exchange-hosted OCC memo specifies an effective date of **19 September 2016** and a deliverable containing XLF shares, distributed XLRE shares and any cash for fractional XLRE shares. The contemporaneous BOX exchange memo identifies an ex-distribution date of 19 September, record date of 21 September and payable date of 22 September. Those documents leave the final distribution ratio undetermined in the versions inspected. [OCC memo on MIAX](https://www.miaxglobal.com/sites/default/files/alert-files/XLF_Distribution_39575.pdf), [BOX exchange memo](https://boxoptions.com/assets/BOXOnnMemo203915.pdf)

The issuer's own annual report subsequently confirms **0.139146 XLRE shares per eligible XLF share**, declared **16 September 2016**, with shareholders of record **21 September** and payment on **22 September**. Its reported total-return convention reinvests distributions at NAV on payment dates; that is a separate convention to check against the provider's adjusted-close series. S&P's later methodology gives a **USD4.44356** reference value; this does not establish a cash election. Do not infer raw-share treatment from a Yahoo split field. [Issuer annual report, financial-statement notes page 94 and return convention page 93](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1064641/000119312516786454/d235287dncsr.htm), [S&P corporate-action example, printed page 33](https://www.spglobal.com/spdji/jp/documents/methodologies/methodology-enhanced-cov-call-strat-indices.pdf)

## What the free-data screen can establish

The existing daily engine archives yfinance DataFrames and metadata, not Yahoo's original HTTP bytes or historical revision vintages. `Adj Close / Close` converts OHLC into fractional adjusted research units; it does not reconstruct raw shares, settlement cash, actual dividend reinvestment or the treatment of an in-kind distribution. Daily bars do not contain opening-auction depth, bid/ask spreads, trade conditions or execution priority. A fixed-bps model measures sensitivity to assumed costs; it is not evidence that those historical fills were available.

Before interpreting a completed study, inspect the frozen XLF rows and all action columns around September 2016, including how the provider represents the event and the adjustment-factor change. A numerically smooth adjusted series alone is not independent certification of correct total return. Raw-share promotion requires final issuer distribution terms, entitlement and payment dates, the resulting XLRE position or explicit disposal rule, cash-in-lieu treatment, fees and reconciliation against an independently sourced total-return calculation. The current adjusted-index proxy must remain labelled `research_only` until these prerequisites are satisfied.

The issuer publication discusses trading costs separately from fund expenses and uses a dated, illustrative spread estimate. It cannot calibrate 2000–2022 opening-auction costs. ETF historical market prices already reflect fund-level expenses economically; adding today's expense ratio to those returns would double count costs and apply the wrong historical fee schedule. Additional investor commissions, spreads, impact and taxes require separate assumptions. [Issuer cost discussion, pages 1–2 and fund-risk disclosures](https://www.ssga.com/library-content/pdfs/etf/us/target-a-lower-cost-of-ownership-with-sector-etfs.pdf)

## Implementation acceptance checks

- Request only **2000–2022** prices, with exact original symbols and adjustment settings. Reject unavailable, duplicate, unordered or extra sessions rather than silently shifting dates or substituting an index/fund.
- Preserve immutable request, response, action columns, metadata, retrieval timestamps, source/dependency versions, quality report and checksums. An action count is a diagnostic, not an independent completeness certificate.
- For offset `L`, require at least `L + 1` completed observations. The declared skip rule is `close[t-21] / close[t-L] - 1`; for `L=252`, Python positional indexing is `history.iloc[-22] / history.iloc[-253] - 1`. It is not `history.iloc[-22] / history.iloc[-274] - 1`.
- The repository calendar has **252** sessions in 2000 and **273** through 31 January 2001. At that first training month-end, the 252-session denominator is **1 February 2000** and the skipped-month numerator is **29 December 2000**. These are calendar-only checks, without price acquisition. `MarketCalendar.sessions('2000-01-01', ...)` currently raises an out-of-bounds error because the configured first session is 3 January; use the exact first requested session, not a silently shifted date.
- Apply targets only at the following official session open. Ties retain frozen universe order; no short positions, borrowing or gross target above one. The benchmark must obey the same capital, timing, cost and end-valuation conventions.
- Perturb data strictly after a signal and verify unchanged earlier decisions/fills. Replay a frozen dataset and reconcile every cash, unit, cost and valuation mark independently. Include known-answer skipped-month, tie, cost and XLF-event fixtures.

## Interpreting a completed study

Compare the unchanged primary rule against both equal-weight sectors and SPY over identical monthly labels. The paired-bootstrap helper now requires identical daily date labels before constructing monthly returns. Report historical compounded growth separately from annualized mean log-return difference; the latter is not factor alpha.

Use the predeclared 12-month moving blocks and show the known search family of ten configurations, including the previous six. This is an approximate dependence-aware uncertainty calculation, not a correction for unknown external research or retrospective universe selection. Two benchmarks and three cost scenarios are correlated views of the same price path, not six independent confirmations. A ten-year validation contains only ten nonoverlapping one-year blocks; fine tail quantiles from 5,000 draws should not be presented as precise significance.

Report concentration, average equity exposure, volatility, maximum drawdown, turnover, costs and chronological subperiod excess alongside return. A market-beta or static-sector-exposure diagnostic may explain a result but must not become a new post-validation selection rule. Log every diagnostic as an exploratory attribution step if it was not frozen in the protocol. Reject an edge claim if apparent excess is explained by risk/exposure, vanishes under plausible costs or depends on one episode. A positive screen remains inconclusive pending independent-source replication, executable corporate-action accounting, a locked reserved-period evaluation and genuinely forward simulated observations.

A smooth price path does not make the synthetic split representation a real XLF share-count split. Issuer market returns use the bid/offer midpoint at NAV valuation and NAV distribution reinvestment, so this comparison cannot isolate an adjustment error. It remains unresolved and is not full independent-source replication. ## Source preservation and contamination disclosure

The public source links remain the verification references.

During corporate-action searches, a search engine returned an unrelated third-party snippet containing 2023–2025 annual fund-return figures despite domain terms in the search text. That result was not opened or used, and no reserved historical price series was requested or evaluated. Nevertheless, claims about a completely unknown final period must disclose incidental human/LLM historical knowledge. Further searches must use explicit domain filters. The reserved dataset boundary remains a reproducibility control, not proof that the researchers have no contextual knowledge of the period.
